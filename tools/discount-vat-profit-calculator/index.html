Formulas Explained: Discount, VAT Extraction, Margin vs. Markup & F-Commerce Net Profit

Accurate pricing is the difference between a profitable online store and selling at a hidden loss. Many Facebook commerce (F-commerce) and Daraz/Shopify merchants calculate gross product profit (`Selling Price - Sourcing Cost`) without accounting for Steadfast/Pathao/RedX courier fees, bubble-wrap packaging, Facebook ad cost per conversion, and COD parcel return losses. ToolNest gives you transparent formulas for every scenario:

1. Discount & Stacked Discount Formula

Discount = Price × (Discount% ÷ 100)
After 1st = Price − Discount
2nd Discount = After 1st × (Extra% ÷ 100)

2. Add VAT vs. Remove Inclusive VAT

Add VAT: Gross = Net × (1 + Rate/100)
Remove VAT: Net = Gross ÷ (1 + Rate/100)
VAT Amount = Gross − Net

3. Profit Margin % vs. Markup %

Gross Profit = Selling − Cost
Margin % = (Gross Profit ÷ Selling) × 100
Markup % = (Gross Profit ÷ Cost) × 100
Reverse Selling Price: Cost ÷ (1 − Target Margin%)

4. F-Commerce Net Profit Formula

Net Profit = Gross Profit − (Delivery + Packaging + Ad Cost + Return Allowance)
Net Margin % = (Net Profit ÷ Selling) × 100
