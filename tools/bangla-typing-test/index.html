  1. Step 1 — Pick Difficulty & Time Limit: Select Easy, Medium, or Hard (Conjuncts) and choose a 1, 2, or 5 minute test duration. Click New Passage anytime for a fresh paragraph.
  2. Step 2 — Switch Keyboard to Bangla & Start Typing: Enable your Bangla Unicode keyboard (Avro, Bijoy Unicode, or Probhat) and begin typing in the box. The countdown starts automatically on your first keystroke.
  3. Step 3 — Review Your WPM & Accuracy: Watch live character highlights as you type, and review your final WPM, accuracy percentage, and error breakdown when time expires.

How Bangla WPM & Conjunct-Safe Accuracy Are Calculated

Many generic online typing tests are built only for ASCII English characters and fail when testing Bengali because a single Bangla syllable or conjunct (Juktoborno / যুক্তবর্ণ) is composed of multiple Unicode code points (base consonant + hasant ্ + second consonant + vowel sign). ToolNest's Bangla Typing Test is engineered specifically for Bengali Unicode scripts.

How WPM Is Calculated

Words Per Minute (WPM) compares your typed words against the passage words after Unicode NFC normalization:
WPM = Correct Bangla Words ÷ Elapsed Minutes.

How Accuracy % Is Calculated

Accuracy % compares normalized Unicode characters:
Accuracy = (Correct Characters ÷ Total Typed Characters) × 100.

Unicode NFC & Nukta Unification

Some keyboards output decomposed ড + ় while others output precomposed ড়. Our engine normalizes both strings to NFC and unifies nukta pairs so you never get false errors.
