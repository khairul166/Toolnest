How to Create a robots.txt File

  1. Step 1 — Pick a Preset or User-Agent: Choose one of the quick presets (such as Block Admin Only, WordPress Default, or SEO Friendly) or enter a specific crawler User-agent like * or Googlebot.
  2. Step 2 — Add Disallow, Allow & Sitemap Rules: Enter the folder paths you want crawlers to skip or allow, and paste your full XML sitemap URL.
  3. Step 3 — Copy & Upload to Your Site Root: Click Copy and save the text as robots.txt in your website's root folder (e.g., https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt).
