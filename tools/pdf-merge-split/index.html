How to Merge, Reorder, or Split PDF Files

  1. Step 1 — Upload One or More PDFs: Drag and drop your PDF files into the upload zone (or click Load Sample 5-Page PDFs to test immediately).
  2. Step 2 — Arrange Pages or Specify a Range: Use the Up (), Down (), and Remove () buttons to customize page sequence, or type a page range like 1-3, 5 in the Split box.
  3. Step 3 — Download Your New PDF: Click Download Merged PDF to combine everything or Extract Page Range PDF to save only your chosen pages.

Why Local Browser PDF Merging Is Safer for Confidential Documents

Whether you are assembling bank statements, TIN tax certificates, trade licenses, academic transcripts, or property deeds, your PDF files contain sensitive personal and financial records. Traditional cloud PDF mergers upload your documents to remote servers where files may be cached or logged.

ToolNest uses the open-source pdf-lib engine inside your web browser. When you select PDF files on this page, your browser reads the binary page streams directly from your device's memory and constructs the new PDF locally—zero bytes are ever uploaded over the internet.
