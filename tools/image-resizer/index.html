How to Resize Images for Facebook & Social Media

  1. Step 1 — Upload Your Photo: Drag and drop your image into the upload box or click Select Image.
  2. Step 2 — Choose a Preset or Custom Size: Click any of the one-click Facebook buttons (Post, Cover, Story, Ad) or enter your own exact width and height in pixels.
  3. Step 3 — Pick Fit Mode & Download: Choose between Cover (crops edges cleanly), Contain (adds clean background padding), or Stretch, then click Download Resized Image.
