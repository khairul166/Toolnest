How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Step 1 — Enter Your Date of Birth: Pick your birth year, month, and day in the Date of Birth calendar input.
  2. Step 2 — Set the Reference Date: By default, the tool sets Age at the Date of to today's date. If you are applying for a government job or school admission with a specific circular cutoff date, select that date instead.
  3. Step 3 — View & Copy Your Exact Age: Instantly read your exact age in years, months, days, weeks, and Bangla wording, or click Copy Result to paste it into your application form.

Why You Need an Exact Chronological Age Calculator

Calculating exact age by hand seems simple until you account for months with 28, 29, 30, and 31 days and leap years occurring every four years. In Bangladesh, public service recruitment exams (such as BCS, Bangladesh Bank, NTRCA, primary school teacher recruitment, and defense services) require candidates to state their exact age in Years, Months, and Days as of a specific gazetted circular date.

Even a one-day miscalculation on an official Teletalk or ministry application portal can create complications during viva verification. ToolNest's Age Calculator uses the standard chronological calendar method recognized by HR departments, passport offices, and admission boards worldwide.
