How to Create a PDF & Organize Pages

  1. Step 1 — Upload Images & PDF Files: Drag and drop any combination of JPG, PNG, WebP photos and existing PDF documents into the upload zone (or click Load Sample PDF + Images to try it immediately).
  2. Step 2 — Visually Organize Pages Like Adobe Acrobat: View live thumbnails for every page. Drag cards to rearrange their sequence, rotate sideways scans 90°, insert blank pages or additional files after any page, inspect pages in the zoom viewer, or delete unwanted pages.
  3. Step 3 — Create or Extract Your PDF: Choose your page size and margin settings, then click Create PDF (All Pages) to build the complete document or Export Selected Pages Only to extract checked pages.
