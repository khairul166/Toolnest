How to Convert MS Word (.DOCX) to PDF

  1. Step 1 — Upload Your Word Document: Drag and drop your .docx, .doc, or .rtf file into the upload box above, or click Upload Word File.
  2. Step 2 — Automatic Conversion: The converter immediately reads your Word document's page dimensions, margins, headings, bold/italic styles, alignments, lists, and tables and converts it into a matching PDF file.
  3. Step 3 — Download Your PDF: Click Download PDF to save the converted PDF file directly to your device.

Why Convert Word Documents to PDF Locally in Your Browser?

Sending a raw .docx file to a client, recruiter, or government portal often leads to broken layouts when the recipient opens the file on a different operating system or without your installed fonts. Converting your Word document into a standardized PDF (Portable Document Format) locks in your pagination, margins, and typography across every phone, tablet, and desktop.

With ToolNest, you never have to upload confidential employment contracts, legal drafts, or academic papers to a third-party server. Our browser engine unpacks the OpenXML structure of your .docx file locally using JSZip, reads the document's original page setup, styles, and tables, and builds an exact multi-page vector PDF using pdf-lib.
