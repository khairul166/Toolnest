How to Make a Passport Photo & Printable Sheet

  1. Step 1 — Upload Your Portrait: Choose a front-facing photo taken in even lighting against a plain wall, or upload a transparent-background PNG.
  2. Step 2 — Select Country Size & Align Face: Choose Bangladesh Passport (35 × 45 mm), US Visa (2 × 2 inch), or Stamp Size. Use the Zoom and Position sliders so your head fits neatly inside the blue oval guide.
  3. Step 3 — Pick Background & Download: Click Pure White or Studio Sky Blue, then download either the Single Photo for online portals or the Printable 4R / A4 Sheet to print 8 to 20 copies for pennies at any local photo lab.

Official Passport, Visa & Stamp Size Specifications

Getting passport and visa photos printed at a studio often costs 150 to 300 BDT for just four copies, and digital copies frequently have the wrong pixel dimensions for online visa portals. With ToolNest's Passport & Visa Photo Maker, you can prepare compliant 300 DPI digital photos and multi-copy 4R sheets in under a minute from your phone or laptop.

Exact 300 DPI Calibration

Outputs exact 413×531 px for 35×45mm (Bangladesh, UK, Schengen) and 600×600 px for 2×2 inch (US & Indian Visa).

Automatic 4R & A4 Tiling

Arranges 8 photos on a 4×6 inch (4R) sheet or up to 20 photos on A4 paper with crisp scissor cut lines.
