How to Generate an Invoice or COD Courier Slip

  1. Step 1 — Fill Merchant & Customer Info: Choose between COD Courier Parcel Slip or Standard Invoice, pick your courier service, and enter the sender and recipient details.
  2. Step 2 — Add Products & Charges: Add product rows, set quantities and prices, and enter delivery charges (e.g. 60 BDT Inside Dhaka / 120 BDT Outside Dhaka) and any advance bKash payment.
  3. Step 3 — Print PDF or Share PNG: Click Print / Save as PDF to print on A4/thermal paper or click Download PNG Receipt to send an instant order confirmation image in Messenger.

Built for Bangladesh F-Commerce Sellers & Global Freelancers

Running a Facebook page shop (F-commerce) or small e-commerce business in Bangladesh means handling dozens of Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders every day through Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, Sundarban, or Paperfly. Customers frequently pay the delivery fee in advance via bKash or Nagad and pay the remaining balance in cash to the delivery rider.

Standard western invoice templates lack fields for courier names, delivery charges, and partial advance deductions—leading to confusion when the courier rider collects cash at the doorstep. ToolNest's Invoice & Courier Slip Generator calculates the exact Collectible COD Balance automatically and displays it in a bold banner.
