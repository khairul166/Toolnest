How to Build a UTM Tracking Link in 3 Steps

  1. Step 1 — Enter Your Landing Page URL: Paste the destination website or product page URL (for example, https://yourstore.com/eid-sale) into the top box, or click a One-Click Channel Preset such as Facebook Ads or Google CPC.
  2. Step 2 — Fill in Your UTM Tags: Enter your traffic source (utm_source), marketing medium (utm_medium), and campaign name (utm_campaign). Optionally add utm_term for audience targeting and utm_content to compare different video or banner creatives.
  3. Step 3 — Copy or Export CSV: Click Copy UTM URL to paste the link directly into Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, or your SMS panel, or click Add to Campaign Batch List to build multiple links and download them as a spreadsheet (.CSV).

Complete Guide to UTM Parameters for GA4 & E-Commerce

When you run Facebook boosts, Instagram Reel ads, YouTube sponsorships, or bulk SMS campaigns in Bangladesh or globally, visitors often land on your website without clear attribution. Social apps like Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, and Instagram frequently strip referrer headers when opening links inside in-app browsers—causing paid traffic to appear as "Direct" traffic inside Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

By attaching standardized UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) parameters to your destination URLs, you guarantee that every click carries explicit source, medium, and campaign metadata directly into your analytics reports and Shopify, WooCommerce, or custom checkout dashboards.

Parameter Status Purpose Real-World Examples
utm_source Required Identifies the platform or referrer sending traffic. facebook, google, instagram, newsletter
utm_medium Required Identifies the marketing channel category for GA4 channel grouping. paid_social, cpc, social, email, sms
utm_campaign Required Names the specific promotion, seasonal sale, or product launch. eid_sale_2026, boishakh_offer, winter_drop
utm_id Optional Links cost data imports in Google Analytics 4. cmp_bd_104, fb_q4_01
utm_term Optional Tracks paid search keywords or Meta ad-set audience segments. buy_panjabi_bd, dhaka_women_22_35
utm_content Optional Differentiates A/B ad creatives, hooks, or button placements. video_reel_a, carousel_slide_2, footer_link

3 Golden Rules for Clean UTM Tracking

  • Always use lowercase: GA4 treats Facebook, facebook, and FB as three separate sources. Keep the Force lowercase switch enabled.
  • Avoid spaces in parameter values: Use underscores (eid_sale_2026) or hyphens (eid-sale-2026) so URLs never break when pasted into SMS messages or chat apps.
  • Never use UTM tags on internal website links: Adding UTM parameters to links between pages on your own site overwrites the original visitor source and resets their session attribution.

Frequently Asked Questions

UTM (Urchin Tracking Module) parameters are short query tags added to the end of a URL (such as utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign). They tell Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and e-commerce dashboards exactly which Facebook ad, email newsletter, or SMS blast brought a visitor to your website.

The three core parameters are utm_source (who sent the traffic, e.g. facebook), utm_medium (the marketing channel, e.g. paid_social or cpc), and utm_campaign (the promotion name, e.g. eid_sale_2026). Optional parameters include utm_id, utm_term (target keyword or audience), and utm_content (specific ad creative or button).

Google Analytics is strictly case-sensitive. It treats utm_source=Facebook and utm_source=facebook as two separate traffic sources. ToolNest includes an automatic Force lowercase toggle enabled by default so your analytics reports stay clean and unified.
