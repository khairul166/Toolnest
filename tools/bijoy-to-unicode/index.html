How to Use the Bijoy to Unicode Converter

  1. Step 1 — Paste Your Source Text: If you have legacy Bangla text written in Microsoft Word or Adobe Illustrator using the SutonnyMJ font, paste it into the top Bijoy Classic box. If you typed Bangla using Avro Keyboard or mobile voice typing, paste it into the bottom Bangla Unicode box.
  2. Step 2 — Convert Instantly: With Instant Live Conversion enabled, the opposite text box updates automatically as you type or paste. You can also click the Bijoy to Unicode or Unicode to Bijoy button at any time.
  3. Step 3 — Copy and Paste Anywhere: Click Copy Unicode Text to paste clean Bangla onto websites, Facebook, WhatsApp, or emails, or click Copy Bijoy Text and apply the SutonnyMJ font in your desktop publishing software.

Key Features & Why Professionals Use This Bangla Converter

In Bangladesh and West Bengal, two major Bangla text encoding standards coexist every day. Official government ministries, court offices, book publishers, and national newspapers frequently prepare manuscripts in Bijoy ANSI (SutonnyMJ) because legacy printing workflows and DTP layout programs depend on ANSI glyph tables. Meanwhile, modern web browsers, smartphones, Google Search, Facebook, and Government e-Service portals exclusively require UTF-8 Bangla Unicode.
