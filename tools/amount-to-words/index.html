How to Convert Numbers & Taka to Words

  1. Step 1 — Type Your Amount: Enter any whole or decimal number into the input field using either English numerals (125500.50) or Bangla numerals (১২৫৫০০.৫০). Commas are ignored automatically.
  2. Step 2 — Select Currency & Cheque Suffix: Choose BDT (Taka & Paisa), USD, INR, GBP, EUR, or Plain Number. Toggle the Append "Only / মাত্র" switch depending on whether you are filling out a bank cheque or an academic document.
  3. Step 3 — Copy Your Wording: Click the Copy button next to the Bangladesh Lakh/Crore box, the Bangla কথায় box, or the International Million/Billion box.

Why Accurate Lakh, Crore & Bangla Wording Matters

When writing a commercial bank cheque, preparing a VAT invoice, or registering property deeds in Bangladesh, even a tiny discrepancy between numeric digits and spelled-out words causes banks to dishonor the cheque. Many international number-to-word calculators only output Hundreds of Thousands and Millions, which are rejected by local bank tellers who require Lakh (লক্ষ) and Crore (কোটি) notation.

Furthermore, spelling compound Bangla numbers between 1 and 99—such as পঁয়তাল্লিশ (45), ঊনপঞ্চাশ (49), তিপ্পান্ন (53), or পঁয়ষট্টি (65)—is notoriously tricky even for native speakers. ToolNest solves both problems simultaneously in a single screen.
