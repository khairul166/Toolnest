How to Convert Excel (.XLSX) to PDF

  1. Step 1 — Select Your Spreadsheet: Drag and drop an Excel workbook (.xlsx) or comma-separated (.csv) file into the upload box, or click Load Sample Excel (.XLSX) to test immediately.
  2. Step 2 — Pick a Worksheet Tab: If your workbook contains multiple sheets (e.g., Monthly Sales, Inventory, Summary), choose the sheet you want to export from the Active Worksheet Tab dropdown.
  3. Step 3 — Customize Page Layout & Theme: Choose A4 or Letter paper size, Landscape (recommended for wide tables) or Portrait orientation, and your preferred header color theme.
  4. Step 4 — Download Vector PDF: Click Download PDF to generate a crisp, searchable multi-page PDF document with repeating table headers and page numbers.

Creating Clean, Printable PDF Reports from Excel Workbooks

Sharing raw .xlsx or .csv files with clients, auditors, or suppliers often leads to accidental formula edits, broken column widths on mobile phones, or exposed hidden worksheets. Converting your worksheet into a standardized A4 Landscape PDF locks in your rows and columns so the table looks identical on every desktop, tablet, and printer.

Our browser-based MS Excel to PDF Converter unpacks OpenXML .xlsx archives directly in memory using JSZip, resolves shared string tables and inline cell values, and draws crisp vector gridlines and typography using pdf-lib. For spreadsheets containing Bangla Unicode text or complex international scripts, you can also click Print / Save Unicode PDF to use your browser's native print-to-PDF engine.

Smart Proportional Column Widths

Automatically measures cell lengths across your dataset so wide product descriptions get more space than short ID columns.

Repeating Table Headers

Multi-page spreadsheets automatically repeat the colored header row at the top of every page for easy reading.
