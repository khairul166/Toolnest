How to Scan & Enhance Documents in Your Browser

  1. Step 1 — Upload a Document Photo: Drop a smartphone photo of any paper page, receipt, NID card, or certificate (or click Load Sample Shadowed Document to see it in action).
  2. Step 2 — Adjust the 4 Corners & Filter: Fine-tune the 4 blue corner handles around the paper edges to flatten the angle. Choose Magic Color to erase desk shadows while keeping colored stamps, or B&W Document for a laser-sharp black-and-white scan.
  3. Step 3 — Export to PDF or Image: Click Download Scanned PDF to get a clean A4/printable PDF, or save as a high-resolution PNG or JPG.

CamScanner-Style Perspective Deskew & Shadow Removal Without Mobile Apps

Taking a photo of a paper document under indoor lighting almost always produces a trapezoid angle and a dark shadow cast by your phone or hand. Submitting raw, gray smartphone photos for bank KYC, job applications, or university portals looks unprofessional and makes text hard to read. ToolNest's AI Document Scanner & Auto-Enhancer uses 4-point homography perspective warping and integral-image local illumination normalization to turn uneven photos into flatbed-quality scans.
