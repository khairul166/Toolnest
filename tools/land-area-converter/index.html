Understanding Land Measurement Units in Bangladesh & South Asia

Whether you are buying an apartment plot in Dhaka, verifying a family land deed (Dalil) or Khatian record (CS, SA, RS, or BS survey), or calculating construction floor area, understanding how traditional South Asian units relate to statutory imperial and metric units prevents costly mistakes.

What Each Land Area Unit Means

  • Square Feet (sq ft / বর্গফুট): The fundamental unit used by architects, real estate developers, and RAJUK/CDA building plans.
  • Square Meter (sq m / বর্গমিটার): The international metric standard where 1 sq m = 10.7639 sq ft.
  • Square Yard (sq yd / বর্গগজ): Equal to a square measuring 3 feet by 3 feet, so 1 sq yd = 9 sq ft.
  • Decimal / Shatak (শতক / ডেসিমেল): The primary legal land survey unit across Bangladesh. Exactly 1/100th of an acre: 1 Decimal = 435.6 sq ft.
  • Katha (কাঠা): Widely used for residential plots. Under the standard official definition, 1 Katha = 720 sq ft (approx. 1.6529 Decimal).
  • Bigha (বিঘা): Traditional agricultural and estate unit equal to 20 standard Kathas: 1 Bigha = 14,400 sq ft (approx. 33.0578 Decimal).
  • Acre (একর): Equals exactly 100 Decimals or 43,560 sq ft (approx. 3.025 Bigha).
  • Hectare (হেক্টর): Metric unit of 10,000 square meters, equal to 107,639 sq ft or approximately 2.471 Acres.

3 Practical Worked Examples

Example 1: Katha to Sq Ft & Decimal

A residential plot in Uttara measures 5 Katha. Using the common standard (1 Katha = 720 sq ft):
5 × 720 = 3,600 sq ft.
In Decimals: 3,600 ÷ 435.6 = 8.2645 Decimal.

Example 2: Decimal (Shatak) to Katha

A rural deed lists 15 Decimal (শতক) of land.
First convert to Square Feet: 15 × 435.6 = 6,534 sq ft.
Divide by 720 sq ft/Katha: 6,534 ÷ 720 = 9.075 Katha.

Example 3: Acre to Bigha & Sq Meter

An orchard covers 2 Acres.
In Square Feet: 2 × 43,560 = 87,120 sq ft.
In Bighas: 87,120 ÷ 14,400 = 6.05 Bigha.
In Sq Meters: 87,120 ÷ 10.7639 = 8,093.72 sq m.
