How to Use the Text Tools & Word Counter

  1. Step 1 — Paste Your Text or List: Enter any blog draft, student essay, phone number list, or keyword sheet into the editor. Word, character, line, and sentence counters update immediately.
  2. Step 2 — Apply One-Click Transformations: Click UPPERCASE, Title Case, Remove Duplicate Lines, or Sort Lines (A → Z). Made a mistake? Click Undo to revert.
  3. Step 3 — Copy or Export: Click Copy Text to copy the cleaned result to your clipboard or Download .txt to save it as a plain text file.
