How to Convert a PDF Table to Microsoft Excel (.XLSX)

  1. Step 1 — Upload Your Tabular PDF: Drop your PDF statement, invoice, or report into the upload area (or click Load Sample Sales Table PDF to test immediately).
  2. Step 2 — Adjust Column Sensitivity & Sheet Layout: Inspect the live spreadsheet preview grid. If needed, switch Column Detection Sensitivity between Tight (8pt), Standard (14pt), and Wide (24pt), or choose whether to combine all pages into one sheet or create a separate worksheet tab per page.
  3. Step 3 — Download .XLSX, .CSV, or Copy: Click Download MS Excel (.XLSX) for a native Excel workbook with numeric cells ready for =SUM() formulas, or click Copy Table to paste directly into Excel or Google Sheets.

Why Local Browser PDF-to-Excel Extraction Is Ideal for Financial Data

Accountants, business owners, auditors, and operations teams frequently receive vendor price lists, courier COD settlement reports, tax schedules, and bank statements locked inside PDF files. Re-typing hundreds of numbers by hand is slow and prone to human error, while uploading confidential financial PDFs to unknown online converters exposes sensitive business numbers.

ToolNest solves both problems by running 100% inside your browser. Using Mozilla's pdf.js engine, the converter reads the exact horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) coordinates of every text item on each page, clusters items on the same horizontal baseline into rows, and separates columns based on spatial gaps. Finally, it constructs a genuine Office Open XML (.xlsx) workbook using JSZip with automatic numeric cell typing.
