How to Password-Protect a PDF

  1. Step 1 — Upload Your PDF: Drag and drop your PDF document into the box above (or click Load Sample PDF to test).
  2. Step 2 — Set Your Open Password & Permissions: Type a strong Document Open Password (or click Generate Strong Password), confirm it, and choose whether recipients can print, copy text, or modify pages.
  3. Step 3 — Encrypt & Download: Click Protect & Download PDF. Your browser encrypts every stream and text object locally and downloads the locked PDF immediately.

Why Encrypt Confidential PDFs Locally in Your Browser?

When sending bank statements, salary slips, tax returns, legal contracts, or NID/passport scans over email or WhatsApp, leaving the PDF unencrypted means anyone who intercepts or forwards the file can read and copy your private data. However, uploading sensitive PDFs and passwords to an unknown online server defeats the entire purpose of security. ToolNest's Protect PDF utility performs 128-bit Standard Security Handler encryption directly inside your browser.

Document Open Password

Requires the exact user password before Adobe Acrobat, Chrome, Edge, or mobile PDF viewers can decrypt and display any page.

Granular Permission Controls

Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Neither your PDF file nor your password is ever transmitted over the internet. Everything stays in your device RAM.

Universal Viewer Compatibility

Uses the ISO 32000-1 Standard Security specification supported across desktop and smartphone PDF readers worldwide.
