How to Batch Resize Multiple Images at Once

  1. Step 1 — Drop Your Image Batch: Drag and drop dozens of photos into the upload box (or click Load 4 Sample Photos to test a multi-image batch immediately).
  2. Step 2 — Pick Your Resize Rule & Naming Pattern: Choose between Exact Dimensions, Target File Size (KB / MB, e.g. 500 KB or 5 MB), Percentage (%), Longest Side, or Fixed Width/Height. Select your crop anchor (e.g. Top Center for portraits), output format (JPG, WebP, PNG), and filename suffix pattern.
  3. Step 3 — Download as a Single ZIP: Click Resize All Queued Images, review the before/after dimensions and file sizes, and click Download All as .ZIP.
