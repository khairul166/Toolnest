How to Make a Digital Signature

  1. Step 1 — Choose Ink & Pen Width: Pick black, blue, or custom ink color and adjust the stroke thickness from 1 px to 16 px.
  2. Step 2 — Draw Your Signature: Sign inside the pad using your mouse, trackpad, finger, or stylus. Use Undo or Clear if you want to retry.
  3. Step 3 — Download PNG or JPG: Keep Transparent enabled and click Download PNG for documents, or click Download JPG for a white-background image.
