Last Updated: October 26, 2026

At ToolNest (accessible from https://[DOMAIN]/), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by ToolNest and how we handle files and data processed through our web utilities.

1. 100% Client-Side File & Text Processing

Unlike cloud-based conversion portals, all utilities on ToolNest—including the Bijoy to Unicode Converter, Amount to Words Converter, Age Calculator, Image Compressor, Image Resizer, Passport Photo Maker, PDF Merge & Split tool, QR Code Generator, Text Tools, and Invoice Generator—operate 100% locally inside your web browser.

Your uploaded photos, biometric portraits, PDF documents, WiFi passwords, and invoice customer records never leave your device and are never transmitted to, stored on, or viewed by our servers.

2. Standard Server Log Files

ToolNest follows a standard procedure of using server log files. These files log visitors when they request static HTML, CSS, or JavaScript assets. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing traffic trends, administering the site, and ensuring technical security.

ToolNest may use cookies to store visitor preferences (such as UI theme settings) and to display relevant advertisements through third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including DoubleClick DART cookies) to serve ads to visitors based upon their visit to https://[DOMAIN]/ and other sites on the internet.
  • Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Third-Party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs)

To ensure fast page loads worldwide, ToolNest loads open-source stylesheets, fonts, and client-side libraries (such as Bootstrap, Google Fonts, pdf-lib, and qrcode-generator) from trusted public CDNs including jsDelivr and Google Fonts. When your browser fetches these static libraries, standard HTTP request headers are processed by those CDN providers under their respective privacy policies.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Because ToolNest does not maintain user accounts or store personal files on our servers, we hold no personal database profiles of our users. You can clear any local browser data or cookies at any time through your web browser settings.
